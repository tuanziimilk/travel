## Answer Drafting Rule

Keep the answer strictly in the language mapped from `country`.
Rewrite from `discount_details`.
Preserve information in this order:
1. Give the conclusion first
2. State which return or refund method is free or not free
3. Add only the single most important limitation

Replace merchant names with `{Mer.}`.
Leave `Source` and `板块名称` blank.
Prefer 1-2 sentences.
Do not call `cancellation policy`, `money-back guarantee`, `free exchange`, `damaged-item replacement`, or `subscription cancellation` by the name `free returns`.
