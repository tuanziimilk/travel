## Answer Drafting Rule

Keep the answer in the language mapped from `country`, not the source language.
Rewrite from `discount_details`.
First classify the source as:
1. direct AAA discount
2. AAA portal cashback
3. AAA travel/member booking benefit
4. non-AAA general offer

Preserve information in this order:
1. Offer type and core benefit
2. Core benefit
3. Key condition

Replace merchant names with `{Mer.}`.
Leave `Source` and `板块名称` blank.
Prefer 1-2 sentences.
Avoid repetitive template sentences.
Do not weaken strong evidence into vague phrases like "may offer" or "other savings may be available".
