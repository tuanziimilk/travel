## Answer Drafting Rule

Keep the answer in the language mapped from `country`.
Rewrite from `discount_details`.
Preserve information in this order:
1. Whether there is a real clearance / outlet / final sale / closeout / sale section
2. If yes, the strongest clearance detail, pricing, or category
3. Key limitation
4. If no, the most common alternative discount type

Replace merchant names with `{Mer.}`.
Leave `Source` and `板块名称` blank.
Prefer 2 sentences.
Do not treat holiday sales, promo codes, bundle offers, app deals, membership pricing, or limited-time discounts as clearance by default.
