## Language Rule

Always output the FAQ in the language mapped from `country`.
Do not follow the source language in `discount_details`.

---

## Question Drafting Rule

Match the question to the actual policy type:

- `price match`: ask whether `{Mer.}` offers an official price match
- `price protection`: ask whether `{Mer.}` offers official price protection
- `low-price guarantee` / `best rate guarantee`: ask whether `{Mer.}` offers a low-price guarantee
- `post-purchase adjustment`: ask whether `{Mer.}` offers post-purchase price adjustment
- mixed or unclear: ask whether `{Mer.}` has an official price guarantee policy

Replace merchant names with `{Mer.}`.

---

## Answer Drafting Rule

Write the answer in 2-3 sentences.
Preserve information in this order:
1. Whether `{Mer.}` offers an official price guarantee / official price match
2. Scope by country, site, store, channel, or product line
3. Key restrictions such as time window, proof, identical item, current price, third-party sellers, excluded categories, and refund method

Do not mix `price match`, `price protection`, `low-price guarantee`, `post-purchase adjustment`, and `store-level discretionary matching`.
When evidence is official and clear, use direct wording such as `offers`, `has`, or `provides`.
When evidence is limited, explicitly frame it as based on current public policy or store-level reports.

Leave `Source` and `板块名称` blank.
