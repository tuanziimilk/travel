## Answer Drafting Rule

Start by judging whether `{Mer.}` has a dedicated existing-customer discount.
If not, make that negative judgment explicit in the target language.
Rewrite from `discount_details`.
Preserve information in this order:
1. Dedicated discount judgment
2. Most valuable mechanism
3. Core benefit
4. Key condition or claim path

Replace merchant names with `{Mer.}`.
Leave `Source` and `板块名称` blank.
Prefer 1-2 sentences.
Prioritize mechanisms such as points, member tiers, birthday coupons, subscriber rates, account-only offers, and app/member pricing.
Avoid repeating the same stock sentence pattern across rows.
