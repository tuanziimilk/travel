## Answer Drafting Rule

Always:
1. Start with `Yes,`, `No,`, or `Not exactly,`
2. Explain the offer mechanism or why it does not qualify
3. Add one more sentence with the key limit, redemption path, or alternative saving action

Keep the answer in the language required by `country`.
Replace merchant names with `{Mer.}`.
Do not treat general promotions, seasonal sales, loyalty pricing, wholesale pricing, auto-renew discounts, or identity-based discounts as a true new customer discount.
When no clear new customer offer exists, include the most common alternative saving path and a next action.
